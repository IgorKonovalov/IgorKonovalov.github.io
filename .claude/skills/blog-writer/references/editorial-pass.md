# Editorial pass: from dictation order to written structure

Added 2026-10-29 after the first reader feedback on the "after-the-team" series (read in
Russian): "interesting, but very hard to read — repetitions, odd grammar and punctuation, and
above all the structure: hard to keep the thread. It reads like a transcript of a video."

## Why it happened

1. Polishing a dictation keeps the order the author spoke in. Speech circles back, digresses
   and repeats for emphasis; on the page that reads as a transcript.
2. The Russian twin was translated from the English post, which was itself made from the
   Russian dictation. Back-translation leaves English syntax and punctuation in Russian.

## The pass (after polishing, before or instead of translating)

**Structure**

- Name the post's one question and its ending. The opening hook must be answered at the end.
- Order by time inside a narrative; by argument inside an argument. No section should need a
  fact that only a later section gives.
- Each thought lives in one place. If a point is made twice in different sections, keep the
  stronger place and cut or reference the other.
- Transitions between sections: one sentence that says why the next section follows.

**Language (Russian)**

- Write Russian from the meaning and the author's dictation, never from the English sentence.
- Join choppy sentences that carry one thought; vary sentence length.
- Cut filler: «очень», «просто», «это», «кажется», «на самом деле», «действительно», «в какой-то
  момент». Keep one where it carries emphasis («очень быстро и очень эффективно»).
- Spoken hedges («кажется… точно не помню») — keep once where they honestly mark a fuzzy
  memory; drop the stacked ones.
- Anaphora from speech («Я не вижу… Я не вижу…») — fold into one sentence unless it is a
  deliberate rhetorical beat.

**What not to do**

- Do not shorten for the sake of it: the posts are personal and the details matter. Remove only
  repetition, never a fact, an anecdote or a feeling.
- Do not add facts. Material may be restored from the polished dictation if the published post
  dropped it — list every such restoration for the author.
- Keep MDX components (charts, Screenshots, Mermaid) verbatim; move them with their text.

## Tightening pass (second reader feedback, 2026-10-29)

The reader, on the closing paragraph of post 3 («Но везение — только одна сторона. У всего,
что я здесь написал про маленькие команды, свободу и быструю работу, есть цена… Следующий
пост, последний, — об этом: чего это стоит мне и чего это может стоить тем, кто только
начинает»): "this sentence broke me — and paragraphs like it, where the point is already made
and then gets decoded once more. By feel, a third of the text would go."

Cut:

- recaps of what the post (or series) already said, and long announcements of the next post —
  one short clause is enough («Но у этой скорости есть цена — о ней последний пост»);
- a closing sentence that repeats the paragraph's opening («Только так», «Так что ретро нужно»,
  «Поэтому двое — магическое число» after it was already said);
- an explanation that decodes the previous sentence, or prose that lists the same steps a chart
  right next to it shows;
- the same point made two or three times in one section — keep the strongest one.

Keep: every fact, number, link, anecdote, and the author's live phrases and feelings («мне
нравилось говорить с людьми», «я люблю ретро», «закладывать уши и рябить в глазах»). The author
tried a one-third cut and found it too harsh; the accepted level is **about 20%** of body words
(post 3 RU: 3,441 → 2,745). Dropping below the length floor is fine here — the floor warns, and
padding back is forbidden.

## English twin

Mirror the new section order and merges in the English version, and remove the same
repetitions. English had no complaints about language — fix structure, not voice.
